What is Phishing?

Understanding the most common cyber threat in the digital world

Phishing is a cybercrime where attackers disguise themselves as trusted entities to trick individuals into revealing sensitive information such as passwords, credit card numbers, or personal data.

Like a fisherman casting bait, cybercriminals craft convincing fake messages — emails, texts, or websites — to "hook" unsuspecting victims. These attacks exploit human psychology rather than technical vulnerabilities.

🎣 Disguises as trusted sources
🧠 Exploits human psychology
⚡ Creates urgency to act fast
🔓 Steals credentials & data
📧

How it Works

Attackers send a message mimicking a legitimate source (bank, IT support, etc.) with a malicious link or attachment.

🎯

The Goal

Steal login credentials, financial data, install malware, or gain unauthorized access to systems.

📊

The Scale

Over 3.4 billion phishing emails are sent every day, making it the #1 cyber attack vector worldwide.

  • 🔴 Creates panic & urgency
  • 🔴 Suspicious external link
  • 🔴 Threatening language

Types of Phishing Attacks

Know your enemy — recognize every form of phishing

📧
MOST COMMON

Email Phishing

Mass fraudulent emails sent from fake domains impersonating banks, services, or IT departments to steal credentials.

Threat Level: HIGH
📱
GROWING

Smishing

SMS-based phishing attacks. Fake text messages claiming to be from delivery services, banks, or government agencies.

Threat Level: HIGH
📞
DECEPTIVE

Vishing

Voice phishing via phone calls. Attackers impersonate tech support, IRS agents, or bank representatives to extract data.

Threat Level: MEDIUM
🎯
TARGETED

Spear Phishing

Highly personalized attacks targeting specific individuals or organizations. Uses personal details gathered from social media.

Threat Level: CRITICAL
🔄
ADVANCED

Clone Phishing

An exact copy of a legitimate email is created with malicious links/attachments, appearing to come from the original sender.

Threat Level: CRITICAL
🐋
EXECUTIVE

Whaling

Spear phishing targeting high-profile executives (CEOs, CFOs). These attacks aim for maximum financial or data impact.

Threat Level: CRITICAL

Fear & Threats

"Your account will be suspended unless you verify NOW." Attackers trigger panic to bypass rational thinking.

⏰

False Urgency

"You have 24 hours to respond!" Artificial time pressure prevents victims from pausing to verify authenticity.

🎭

Impersonation

Pretending to be a CEO, IT admin, bank, or government official to gain trust and compliance.

🎁

False Rewards

"You've won a prize! Click to claim." Greed and curiosity are exploited to trick users into clicking malicious links.

Stay Cyber Safe

Essential practices to protect yourself online

🔗

Verify Links

Always hover over links before clicking. Check the actual URL — look for misspellings or unusual domains.

TIP: Use virustotal.com to scan suspicious URLs
🔐

Enable MFA

Multi-Factor Authentication adds a second layer of security. Even if your password is stolen, attackers can't get in.

TIP: Use authenticator apps over SMS
🔑

Strong Passwords

Use 12+ character passwords with a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Never reuse passwords across sites.

TIP: Use a password manager
⬇️

Safe Downloads

Only download software from official sources. Avoid cracked software — it often contains malware and spyware.

TIP: Scan files with antivirus before opening
📡

Secure Wi-Fi

Avoid entering sensitive data on public Wi-Fi. Use a VPN to encrypt your connection and protect your data.

TIP: Use WPA3 encryption on home networks
🔄

Keep Updated

Regularly update your OS, browser, and apps. Security patches fix vulnerabilities that attackers exploit.

TIP: Enable automatic updates
